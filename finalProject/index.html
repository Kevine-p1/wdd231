Travel Tips

Check seasons

Dry seasons are often best for safaris, trekking, and road travel, but each destination has unique timing.

Respect culture

Learn local customs, dress respectfully in cultural sites, and support local guides and businesses.

Plan transport early

Some parks and remote destinations require advance planning for road transfers, permits, and lodging.
