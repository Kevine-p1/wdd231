East Africa Travel Planner Site Plan

WDD 231 Individual Project Planning Document

Site Name

East Africa Travel Planner

This name clearly communicates the purpose of the website. Visitors immediately understand that the site focuses on discovering destinations and planning travel experiences throughout East Africa.

Optional Domain: eastafricatravelplanner.org

Site Purpose

The East Africa Travel Planner website provides travel information and planning resources for tourists interested in visiting East African countries. The site will feature destination information, travel itineraries, transportation options, accommodation recommendations, weather information, and cultural travel tips.

Target Audience

  • International tourists
  • Regional travelers
  • Adventure seekers
  • Families planning vacations
  • Students interested in cultural tourism

Scenarios

  • What are the most popular tourist destinations to visit in East Africa?
  • What is the best time of year to visit a specific destination?
  • What transportation options are available between major cities and attractions?
  • What cultural experiences can I enjoy during my trip?
